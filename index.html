Southcentral Alaska · Shingle Re-Roof

Old Shingles Off, New Roof On

On this re-roof the worn shingles come off in sections and new underlayment goes straight down behind the tear-off, so the house is never left open to the weather. Then new architectural shingles and a ridge vent finish it off.

  • Tear-off section by section
  • New underlayment laid behind the crew
  • Architectural shingles and ridge vent

Southcentral Alaska · Commercial Metal

Red Metal Over the Fuel Pumps

A gas station and store re-roofed in red metal panels to match the canopy. The crew works tied off in fall-protection harnesses, with stack vents and roof penetrations flashed through the new metal.

  • Through-fastened red metal panels
  • Crew tied off in fall protection
  • Stack vents flashed through the metal

Anchorage · Zero-Lot-Line Homes

Two Homes, One Roofline

Neighbors who share a zero lot line had both homes re-roofed together, so the shared roofline was done as one job. In the owner’s words, it “went GREAT” and Mike and Rudy “did an incredible job for all.”

  • Both homes roofed in one visit
  • One continuous roofline, no mismatched seam
  • Free estimate up front
